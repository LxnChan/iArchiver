## English

Name: iArchiver (availability pending)

Subtitle: Archives, offline and simple

Description:

Import an archive, inspect its contents, extract what you need, or create a new archive. iArchiver processes files on your device, without an account, ads or subscriptions.

- Create ZIP, 7z, TAR, TAR.GZ and GZ archives, and read common formats and ISO9660 images.
- Encrypt ZIP and 7z with AES; encrypt filenames in 7z.
- Choose legacy filename encodings and repack older ZIPs with standard UTF-8 names.
- See previews beside your file list in wide windows, with image and system-supported audio previews.
- Select entries, follow progress, cancel tasks and export results.

The App Store edition provides basic RAR/RAR5 reading; some solid or encrypted methods are unavailable. ISO9660 supports browsing and extraction, without mounting, execution or UDF-only support. RAR creation is unavailable. Encryption and advanced methods require compatible receiving tools.
