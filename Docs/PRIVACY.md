## 简体中文

iArchiver 在你的设备上处理文件。应用不需要账号，不包含广告或使用分析 SDK，不运营文件上传服务器，也不向开发者传送你的文件、压缩包密码或使用记录。

你通过系统文件选择器提供文件或文件夹，或从其他应用打开文件。应用将所选内容复制到应用目录后处理；原文件保留。导出到 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务由你选择，并遵循相应服务自己的条款及隐私政策。

密码仅在当前操作和打开的压缩包上下文中保存在内存。关闭压缩包后清除对应状态；应用不提供自动保存密码的功能。重新打包时，读取密码和输出密码分别设置。

预览和解压会产生本地临时文件，包括解密后的内容。应用将工作目录排除在备份外并设置系统文件保护；完成、失败、取消、关闭预览或过期后清理。异常终止留下的工作目录会在后续启动的过期清理中删除。你保存到应用 Documents 或自行导出的文件由你管理，可能进入你自己的系统备份。

应用仅保存本地偏好，例如安全限额和导出重名策略。任务历史保存在内存，退出进程后不保存。应用使用文件时间、剩余磁盘空间及计时信息来显示文件、检测变化、评估容量和控制处理，没有以这些信息进行跟踪或设备指纹识别。

后台操作可能在系统提供的进度界面显示通用任务名称和进度。应用不需要麦克风、相机、通讯录或位置权限；音频预览只播放你选择的文件。

若你主动联系支持，请勿发送密码或私人文件。你自愿提供的诊断材料和联系信息只应用于处理你的支持请求；发行方应在上线前补充其保存期限、处理渠道及删除请求方式。Apple 的购买、系统诊断和备份行为由相应系统设置及 Apple 政策管理。

## English

iArchiver processes files on your device. It has no account system, advertising or analytics SDK, file upload server, or developer-operated collection of your files, archive passwords or usage history.

You choose files and folders through system document interfaces. The app works on local copies and keeps the originals. Exports to iCloud Drive or another provider are initiated by you and are subject to that provider's policies.

Passwords stay in memory for the active operation or archive context and are cleared when that context is closed. Password saving is unavailable. Repacking uses separate source and output password choices.

Local temporary files may contain decrypted preview or extracted data. Work directories are excluded from backup and use system file protection. They are cleaned after use, failure, cancellation or expiration; expired leftovers from interrupted processes are removed on a later launch. Files you keep in Documents or export remain under your control and may be included in your own backups.

Only local preferences, such as safety limits and export collision handling, are stored. Task history is held in memory. File timestamps, available disk space and elapsed time support file operations and capacity checks, and are not used for tracking or fingerprinting.

System background progress may display a generic operation title and progress. Camera, microphone, contacts and location access are unnecessary. Audio preview plays the files you select.

Please do not send private files or passwords to support. Before publication, the publisher must specify contact details, voluntary support-data handling, retention and deletion requests. Apple purchase, backup and system diagnostic services are governed by their respective settings and policies.
